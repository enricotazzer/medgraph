## Computed eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021)

- eGFR computed for 11,120 usable creatinine values; not computed for 9 (under 18 on the sample date).
- Against the eGFR Synthea reports (MDRD) on the same day: 7,149 pairs; median difference (computed minus reported) -22.2 mL/min/1.73 m2; the two fall on opposite sides of 60 in 2,557 pairs (36%).

**Synthea's reported eGFR does not follow from its own creatinine.** Applying the MDRD Study equation (`mdrd-2006`, without the race factor, which is not recorded) to the same-day creatinine differs from the reported MDRD value by a median of +23.7 mL/min/1.73 m2 (5th to 95th percentile -9.6 to +64.6). The generator models eGFR and creatinine separately. medgraph's criteria use eGFR computed from creatinine, so they are expected to disagree with diagnoses Synthea assigns from its own eGFR.
