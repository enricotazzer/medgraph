Patient graph and timeline

Each node groups records with the same code. Columns, left to right: lab reports, analytes, conditions, medications, procedures. Click a node or a link for its records and sources. Drag to move, scroll to zoom.

Timeline

Drag across a chart to zoom, double-click it to reset. Click a value or an event for its source. A bar runs from start to end; a faded bar has no end recorded. Dots are events on one day; click a group heading to expand or collapse it. Charts show usable values only, and amber points come from a lab report alone; everything set aside is listed under data notes.
